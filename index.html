Wireless World · April to September 1979

PSI COMP80

A Z80 computer designed by J. H. Adams and published as a build-it-yourself series, “A scientific computer”, with a kit from Powertran. It had a 64 × 32 character screen, a calculator chip for its BCD arithmetic, and BURP, a calculator language, in 3 KB of ROM. These pages bring it back: its screens, an emulator that runs the original ROM, annotated listings, a LISP written for it, and its hardware three ways.
