COMP80 Emulator

The PSI COMP80, a Z80 computer published as a build-it-yourself series in Wireless World, running its monitor ROM with one of twenty-one languages and programs in the top 2 KB: the original BURP, or FORTH, LISP, BASIC, Logo, CHIP-8, FOCAL, PILOT, ZASM, Prolog and eleven more under Other, written for this project. Pick a ROM, then click the screen and type, or run one of the examples.

The emulator follows the MAME driver in the psicomp80 repository: a Z80 at 4 MHz, the 3 KB ROM typed in from the magazine’s hex listing, a 64 × 32 character screen and the keyboard on an NMI. It has no cassette or floppy disc; programs that came on tape are loaded straight into memory.
